OHE Compatibility final design validation — 2026-09-07

DESIGN
- P&W owns physical OHE/contact wire and native Energy Wire feeder network.
- CEE owns electrical generation/simulation, transformers/substation/booster and ground.
- OHE Power Bridge is the CEE-to-P&W OHE supply boundary.
- OHE Feeder Bridge is the CEE-to-P&W native Energy Wire feeder boundary.
- TSS Feeder Switch / Isolator is a CEE-native HV switch subclass used upstream of the feeder bridge.
- TSS is an isolator/disconnector, not a circuit breaker; the existing CEE breaker remains upstream protection.
- C1-C4 use the paired sliding runner concept; C1 is the full section-insulator/runner assembly, while C2-C4 retain their own neutral geometry.
- D remains the railway-side OHE/feeder switching assembly.
- Feeder Option 1/2 are physical support options and do not create separate electrical systems.
- CEE ground rod remains on the CEE side.

TSS POWER FLOW
CEE substation/transformer -> CEE breaker -> TSS feeder isolator -> CEE feeder connection -> OHE Feeder Bridge -> P&W native energy_wire.
